Deterministic Optimization Engine Principles

1. Hard Constraints

Never schedules before earliestAllowedDate or after latestAllowedDate. Internal exams remain 100% fixed unless marked reschedulable.

2. Workload Conservation

Total academic effort is strictly conserved (∑ Hours Before == ∑ Hours After). We redistribute, never erase.

3. Peak & Overload Minimization

Applies quadratic penalty to workloads exceeding your daily study threshold to actively squash dangerous workload spikes.

4. Paced Study Distribution

Distributes prep hours across available study sessions before deadlines without exceeding daily availability.
